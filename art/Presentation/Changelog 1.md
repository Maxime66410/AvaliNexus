First release of Avali Nexus!

Here's what's in this first version:

- **Avalis**: packs with their own names, looks and moods, a dialogue overlay, couples, babies, eggs, and companions you can recruit
- **Camps and villages**: nomad camps out in the snow and bigger Avali villages, with a reputation system for each pack
- **Trading**: shops based on each Avali's job or workshop
- **The biome**: a freezing polar biome with blizzards, frozen caves and ammonia lakes, and the cold that slowly freezes you
- **Avalon plants**: Piru, Nakati and Kiri, plus Avali food like the spiced stew that keeps you warm
- **Gear and weapons**: the Nexite tier, aerotech blades and rail guns
- **Building blocks**: graphene, aerogel glass, nanocanvas, ice bricks, packed snow, and tapestries you design on the Avali loom
- **Fluids and machines**: ammonia, coolant and fuel, pipes, tanks, and the refinery, cryo freezer, graphene press, weapon workbench and heater
- **Lore**: the terminal with the Avali archives

Needs GeckoLib, TerraBlender and Mod Menu is optional.

Found a bug? Report it on [GitHub](https://github.com/Maxime66410/AvaliNexus/issues)!
